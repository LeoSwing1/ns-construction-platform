# Choosing a construction contractor

A contractor agreement should remove guesswork before work starts. Screening checklist:

- Verify legal business details, tax information where applicable, address, authorised signatory and banking details.
- Obtain a written scope tied to architectural and structural drawings.
- Confirm who handles approval submissions, fees, revisions and inspections.
- Name the site supervisor or role, reporting method and decision authority.
- Set payment stages against measurable work and inspection records.
- State how incomplete, rejected, damaged or changed work is corrected and priced.
- Record the defect process and warranty terms; list manufacturer warranties separately.
- List labour, materials, testing, debris removal, temporary services, utility connections, taxes and external works.

Ask to inspect completed work and speak with past clients about variation charges, schedule changes, site cleanliness, supervision and after-handover responses.

## Contract items and records

| Item | Record required |
| --- | --- |
| Scope | Signed scope and drawing list |
| Payment | Measurement sheet and inspection sign-off |
| Variations | Written variation order |
| Supervision | Named contact and communication record |
| Handover | Handover checklist |

Delay appointing a contractor while land-use or ownership is unclear, the authority is unconfirmed, the room schedule keeps changing, or quotations use different drawings.
