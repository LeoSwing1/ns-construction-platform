# House construction costs and budgeting

A headline per-square-foot rate is only an early planning signal. A reliable estimate comes from drawings, quantities, specifications, current supplier quotations, site conditions and a clear list of exclusions. Market rates change, so rates should be checked at the time of procurement.

## Estimate headings

- Land, legal, survey and site documentation.
- Design, structural, consultant and approval fees.
- Site preparation, excavation and foundation work.
- Concrete, reinforcement, masonry, roofing and plaster.
- Electrical, plumbing, drainage, tanks and service connections.
- Doors, windows, flooring, tiles, paint, sanitaryware and fixtures.
- External paving, boundary work, drainage and landscaping.
- Temporary services, debris removal, taxes, testing and an allowance for changes.

## Comparing quotations

Compare quotations against the same approved drawings and written specifications. For each rate, mark whether it includes labour, materials, delivery, wastage, taxes, testing and installation. Record how variations are priced. Items commonly left outside a headline price: approvals, soil work, switches, waterproofing, external drainage, taxes and testing.

## Payments

Tie payments to measurable completed work and inspection records, not calendar dates alone.
