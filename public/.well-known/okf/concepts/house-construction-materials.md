# House construction materials

Material choices affect performance, maintenance, appearance and cost. For each material, record the required grade, size, performance requirement, approved equivalent, storage method, inspection responsibility and test record. Lucknow conditions to account for: hot summers, seasonal monsoon moisture, soil conditions and number of floors.

## Material groups to cover

- Cement, concrete inputs, reinforcement steel, sand and aggregates.
- Masonry units, mortar, plaster and waterproofing systems.
- Plumbing pipes, valves, tanks, drainage components and sanitaryware.
- Electrical cables, conduits, switchgear, earthing, switches and fixtures.
- Doors, windows, glass, hardware, tiles, adhesives, paint and sealants.

## Delivery and testing

At delivery check quantities, visible damage, batch details and storage. Protect cement from moisture. Before concealment, record plumbing pressure tests, electrical checks, waterproofing tests and photographs of service routes. Use project drawings and engineer approval before selecting a grade or accepting an equivalent.

Reference guidance: [BIS Guide for Homeowners and Homebuyers](https://www.bis.gov.in/guide-for-homeowners-and-homebuyers/?lang=en) and the [National Building Code of India](https://www.bis.gov.in/standards/national-building-code/?lang=en).
