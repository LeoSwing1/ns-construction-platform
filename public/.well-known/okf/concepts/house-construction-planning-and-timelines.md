# House construction planning and timelines

A credible timeline is built around dependencies, not a fixed duration. Duration depends on design, site conditions, approval route, contractor scope, labour, supply and weather.

## Dependencies

- Approved drawings before execution.
- Material lead times.
- Curing and testing time.
- Inspection access.
- Utility coordination.
- Prompt owner decisions (for example window selection before masonry reaches the openings).

## Stages and triggers

| Stage | Inspection or payment trigger |
| --- | --- |
| Design and approval | Approved submission and signed drawing set |
| Procurement | Purchase records and approved samples |
| Foundation | Inspection before concrete placement |
| Structure and enclosure | Measured work and stage inspection |
| Services and finishes | Concealed-work tests and finish approval |
| Testing and handover | Signed handover and agreed outstanding list |

Decisions on stair locations, plumbing shafts, toilet layouts, electrical points, windows and structural members belong near the beginning; changing them later causes demolition, new material and delay costs.
