# House plan approval in Lucknow

Before approving a final plan, confirm the plot's applicable authority, land-use status, access, setbacks, permissible height, floor-area rules and parking provisions. Rules, fees and documents must be confirmed with the relevant authority at the time of application.

## Official sources

- [Lucknow Development Authority building bylaws](https://www.ldalucknow.in/building-bylaws/)
- [Uttar Pradesh Online Building Plan Approval System](https://upobpas.in/BPAMSClient/Home.aspx)
- [Uttar Pradesh Model Building Bylaws 2025](https://sdma.up.gov.in/assets/uploads/geophysical/1770960782_UP%20Model%20Building%20Bylaws%202025%20English.pdf)

## Typical document checklist

The exact list depends on plot, location and proposal; confirm it with the authority.

- Ownership and title papers requested by the authority.
- Current site measurements, boundaries and plot information.
- Architectural drawings and application forms.
- Structural documents where required.
- Owner identity and contact details.
- Fee receipts, application records and file numbers.

A contractor may prepare drawings or track a submission if the agreement says so, but cannot replace the authority's review or guarantee approval.
