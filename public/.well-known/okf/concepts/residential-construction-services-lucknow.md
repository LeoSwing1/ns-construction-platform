# Residential construction services in Lucknow

Residential construction services cover building a house from scope definition to handover. A sound engagement has a defined scope, verified approvals, comparable quotations, written material specifications, stage inspections and a controlled handover.

## Contract models

- **Design and build:** one company coordinates design and execution. Confirm who owns the drawings and signs structural decisions.
- **Labour contract:** the contractor supplies labour; the owner or another party buys materials.
- **Material-inclusive (turnkey):** the contractor manages labour and procurement within a defined specification.

The written scope controls, not the label.

## Construction sequence

1. Site inspection and design brief.
2. Soil investigation where required.
3. Architectural and structural drawings, specifications and quantity estimates.
4. Approval submission.
5. Mobilisation and procurement.
6. Foundations, frame, walls, roof, doors and windows.
7. Services (plumbing, electrical, waterproofing) installed before covering.
8. Finishes and external works.
9. Testing, snag list and handover.
