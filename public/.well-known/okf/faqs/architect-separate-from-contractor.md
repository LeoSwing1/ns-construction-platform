# Should I hire an architect separately from a construction company in Lucknow?

It depends on the contract model. An architect can independently develop or review the design, coordinate drawings and support inspections, while a construction company executes the agreed work. Some firms coordinate both functions.

Either way, the contract must identify design responsibility, structural responsibility, approval duties and inspection authority. See [choosing a construction contractor](../concepts/choosing-a-construction-contractor.md).
