# Can a construction company in Lucknow handle building plan approval on the owner's behalf?

Yes, for coordination. A construction company can coordinate drawings and submissions when the owner authorises it and the contract assigns that responsibility. The approving authority reviews and decides the application, so the contractor cannot replace the approval process or guarantee acceptance.

Put responsibility for preparation, corrections, fees and communication in writing. See [house plan approval in Lucknow](../concepts/lucknow-house-plan-approvals.md).
