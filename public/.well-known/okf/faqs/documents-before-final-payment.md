# What documents should I ask for before making the final payment to a house construction contractor?

Request:

- the signed handover checklist;
- marked-up or as-built drawings;
- approval records;
- test results;
- product manuals and applicable warranties;
- keys and meter details;
- invoices and variation approvals;
- a written list of outstanding defects or work.

The signed agreement controls which documents are due. Record incomplete work in a signed list and state whether any amount remains payable. See [residential construction services](../concepts/residential-construction-services-lucknow.md).
